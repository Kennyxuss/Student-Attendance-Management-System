# Week 10 – Board Lead Contribution

**Project:** Student Attendance Management System  
**Role:** Board Lead – Demelyn Concepcion

## Contribution

As the Board Lead, I helped organize and track the team's Manual QA and Bug Hunting activities through the GitHub Project Board and issue tracker.

Week 10 focused on feature freeze, manual QA, adversarial testing, and documenting and triaging bugs rather than immediately fixing them.

## Responsibilities

1. **Organize QA tasks** – Help make sure QA activities are represented and tracked on the project board.
2. **Track testing progress** – Monitor the test matrix, manual QA passes, automated coverage for critical gaps, adversarial testing, and bug documentation.
3. **Track bugs as Issues** – Help ensure every discovered bug has a title, reproduction steps, expected result, actual result, and severity.
4. **Monitor severity** – Track P0 (core/data loss), P1 (significant with workaround), and P2 (minor) issues.
5. **Monitor board status** – Keep task status updated and follow up when updates are needed.
6. **Coordinate with members** – Communicate with team members about QA tasks and bug reports.

## Week 10 Deliverables

- `/docs/test-matrix.md`
- Expanded automated tests for critical gaps
- Adversarial testing
- Bug Issues with reproduction steps and P0/P1/P2 labels
- `/docs/ai-notes/week-10.md`

## Summary

My main contribution was to **organize, track, and coordinate the QA and bug-hunting work**. I did not need to fix every bug myself; instead, I helped make sure discovered problems were properly documented, prioritized, and visible on the project board.
